Note: why register?

Your account gives you a user id. The app uses it to link every booking to you.

After you register, the badge at the top shows who you are signed in as.

Add a pickup slot (admin)

Note: what is a slot?

A slot is one time window when a collector visits one area, for example 2026-11-02, 10:00 to 12:00, Andheri.

This form is for admins. Residents only book the slots created here.

Note: booking a slot

Press Book on any row to reserve it. You must register first.

Once a slot is booked it disappears from this list, so two people can’t take the same one. Cancelling brings it back.
